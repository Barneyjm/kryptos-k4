What the evidence pins down

Hard constraints

  • Cribs24 letters: EASTNORTHEAST at 22–34, BERLINCLOCK at 64–74. About 113 bits of evidence, enough to confirm any hand-cipher key.
  • Sanborn"One-to-one": each letter is encrypted in place. No reordering.
  • 42 apartPositions 24 and 66 are both V (from T and L); 31 and 73 are both K (from A and K). No scheme may reuse one substitution 42 positions apart, so periods 7, 14, 21 and 42 are impossible for any alphabets.
  • K→KPosition 74 encrypts to itself, which rules out ciphers that can't (Porta, Enigma-like).
  • Scheidt"More than one stage", "change the language base", "simple, can be remembered… with the correct key word/s".

Statistical fingerprints

  • Width 2111 repeated vertical pairs: 1 in ~210 after correction. It's mostly the stacked doublets.
  • Width 75 of 6 doublets in one column: 1 in 2,500 if 7 is pre-chosen (KRYPTOS), 1 in 4 if not.
  • DoubletsEvery visible column-5→6 step is a double: NO→QQ, ST→SS, IN→TT (plaintext gaps +1, +1, +5).
  • Minor diffsBean's near-A–Z effect: p = 0.14 using all 24 letters, so not significant.
  • CiphertextIC 0.036, entropy 4.55 bits/letter (the random ceiling; K1/K2 4.35–4.39, K3 3.95).
  • Implied keyRandom-like linear complexity under 8.4M alphabet variants. A short memorable key would need a nonlinear expansion step.

Gaps, ranked

Ranked by plausibility for a 1989 hand cipher and by the cost of testing.

  1. Sanborn-phrase alphabets × interrupted keys, clock streams, Chaocipher

    The 613k phrase alphabets are now closed for periodic, digit, running, linear and autokey structures. The three newest families were run with clue and dictionary alphabets only.

  2. Chaocipher, full dictionary × dictionary

    About 10¹³ rows. A 1M random sample and the whole clue × dictionary block are at the shuffled baseline, and Byrne's rule was unpublished in 1989.

    weeks; low prior

  3. Two stages with longer periods or three stages

    Two tableaux with L1, L2 ≤ 12 sit at baseline. Longer periods are mostly undecidable with 24 cribs, and three stages are out of reach.

    days; mostly undecidable

  4. Unpublished material

    Strip-cipher sheets, arbitrary non-keyword alphabets, one-time key sheets. Nothing to search against.

    blocked

  5. Undecidable cells

    Mask with key lengths 13, 16, 19, 20, 23, 24, 26; both alphabets free with long keys; mask + grid keys at widths above ~11. These need more known plaintext than 24 letters.
